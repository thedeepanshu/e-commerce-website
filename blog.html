How to Choose the Right Running Shoes

The right pair of shoes can make every run more comfortable. Here's what to look for before you buy.

Read more

Start with how you run. If your feet roll inward when they land, look for a stability shoe; if they land neutrally, a cushioned neutral shoe is usually the best fit.

Shop later in the day, when your feet are slightly swollen, and leave about a thumb's width of space at the toe. Replace running shoes every 500 to 800 km, as the cushioning wears out long before the upper looks tired.

Getting Started with Road Cycling

Thinking about swapping the gym for the open road? These basics will help you ride further with confidence.

Read more

Get your bike fitted: saddle height alone can prevent most knee pain. A helmet, lights and a bright jersey are non-negotiable for safety.

Begin with short, flat routes and build your distance by around 10% a week. Ride with a group when you can; it's the fastest way to learn and the best way to stay motivated.

Build a Training Routine You'll Stick With

Consistency beats intensity. Here's how to create a weekly plan that fits your life.

Read more

Pick a realistic number of sessions, say three a week, and put them in your calendar like any other appointment. Mix strength, cardio and mobility so your body gets a balanced workload.

Track your sessions, celebrate small wins, and schedule at least one full rest day. Recovery is when your body actually gets stronger.
